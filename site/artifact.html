When the model called a Swannanoa home Major or Destroyed, Buncombe County’s Helene search teams agreed 15 times out of 19.

It also misses a lot: it found 15 of the 64 homes the teams called Major or Destroyed. Flood damage inside a house does not show from above. That is why people check.

178 homes seen by both. Not an accuracy figure: an area used for tuning, not a held-out test. The teams logged mostly visibly damaged homes in the first weeks after landfall; the imagery is from months later.

GROMANamed for the Roman surveyor’s instrument, which let gravity decide what was straight.

Groma is decision support. It is not an official FEMA or state emergency management damage assessment, and its accuracy is not yet validated. Verify on the ground.

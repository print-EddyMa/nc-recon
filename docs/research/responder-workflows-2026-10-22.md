# How responders work, and what Groma must give them (research, 2026-10-22)

## Points that change the product

1. **The "damage level" scale has to map to more than one scale.** FEMA PDA (Aug 2021 Pocket Guide) uses **Affected / Minor / Major / Destroyed** and has *dropped "Inaccessible"* as a damage degree. During Helene, the SARCOP field teams (US&R and swiftwater) used **six** levels: Unaffected, Affected, Minor, Moderate, Major, Destroyed. The tool should ship an explicit crosswalk and keep **access/inaccessible as a separate flag**, never a damage class.
2. **"Not assessed" must roll up as GREY, never green.** In the FEMA Lifelines Toolkit 2.1 (p.25), Grey means "extent of disruption... is unknown". Every area summary needs a coverage % (buildings assessed / buildings present).
3. **Overhead imagery cannot see interior waterlines.** Interior water depth drives Minor vs Major in flood PDAs; the manufactured-home matrix puts a 3–18 in waterline in "major or minor" depending on conditions. The product must say this, and flood calls should be framed as "candidate for field verification".
4. **FEMA's per-building debris formula is for *destroyed/demolished* homes only.** No FEMA per-damage-level coefficient exists in FEMA 329. The Helene-relevant number is curbside flood personal property (25–30 CY slab, 45–50 CY basement).
5. **The Lifelines "Impacts" field explicitly asks for "impacted areas and population totals"** (Toolkit p.23). Building counts by damage level per area, with residents, map directly to it.

## Workflow notes

- **US&R** triages structures to get "the most saves in shortest time" before a full search, because a full search takes hours. The Structures Specialist does the triage. Priority is driven by occupancy, **time of day**, construction type and collapse pattern (FEMA US&R Rescue Specialist manual App A, fema.gov/pdf/emergency/usr/appen_a.pdf; NFPA 1006 ch.6). The 2×2 ft structure-hazard box has three levels. The search mark is one slash on entry and a crossing slash on exit, giving an X. The contents of the four X quadrants are UNVERIFIED against the current FOG (2003 FOG: dco.uscg.mil/.../Nat.UrbanSAR_fog25sept2003.pdf). For hurricanes, teams do **wide-area search** sector by sector and record per-structure damage in SARCOP/Survey123.
- **Guard IAA** products: full-motion video and stills from RC-26 (EO/IR, ROVER downlink), plus damage and route recon (twz.com Harvey article; nationalguard.mil). Every imagery use is bounded by the PUM, which allows no collection on specific US persons. Practical rule for this tool: building-level outputs, no person or vehicle tracking, and record each product's purpose. Sectorizing an AO by USNG 1 km / 10 km squares is common practice but the cell-size convention is UNVERIFIED.
- **Insurers** run Geospatial Insurance Consortium aerial imagery through ML to flag affected properties, find total losses and route field adjusters. Examples: Brit's virtual adjusting, Verisk/Vexcel for Ian, Munich Re (verisk.com newsroom; insurancebusinessmag.com).

## Requirements by audience ("needs X, at Y, within Z")

**EOC / Emergency management**
- Needs counts of Destroyed / Major / Minor / Affected / unassessed buildings, with residents (pop./household proxy), at county, municipality and Division-polygon level as GeoJSON + CSV + PDF map, **within the first 12–24 h operational period** and refreshed every operational period.
- Needs a lifeline-style rollup (Status / Impacts with area + population / Limiting factors) where unassessed = Grey, with a coverage %. It should be **ready before each Situation Unit cycle** so it can drop into the Tier III / IAP Incident Map.
- Needs the PDA package per county: homes by degree of damage, estimated cost-of-assistance inputs, owner/renter split, an insurance-penetration placeholder, a population-profile join (Census %≥65, poverty, disability, LEP), and a PA countywide check (est. $ ÷ population vs **$4.86**). Format is a spreadsheet matching state PDA forms, **within 72 h**, because the governor's request is built from it.
- Needs debris estimates in CY and tons by Division using the FEMA 329 rules (destroyed homes with VCM; mobile homes 290/415 CY; flood curbside 25–50 CY per affected home), labelled ±30% planning-grade, **within 72 h** for Category A contracts.

**National Guard / DSCA**
- Needs every building, sector and LZ referenced in **USNG/MGRS (10-digit, 1 m)**, plus a USNG-gridded sector overlay (KML/GeoPDF) usable offline on a laptop, **for the first tasking (0–24 h)**.
- Needs candidate helicopter landing points screened against **50 m (UH-60) / 80 m (CH-47) clear diameter, slope ≤7° (≤15° absolute), obstacles ≥18 in flagged, 10:1 obstacle ratio on approach**, from DEM + imagery. Each comes as a point with diameter, slope, obstacle notes and USNG, **before air tasking each day**.
- Needs route status (open / limited / closed, washouts, bridge out) as line features approximating the route-classification fields (width, X/Y/Z, obstructions), **within 24 h**.
- Needs products that fit under a PUM: no person or vehicle detection, imagery provenance and purpose recorded.

**US&R**
- Needs a structure-triage list ranking buildings by damage level × likely occupancy (residential at night, commercial and school by day) × construction type, at single-building resolution with USNG and address, **within hours of the first imagery**.
- Needs wide-area-search sector polygons (Divisions) with building counts and search-status fields compatible with SARCOP/Survey123 (FeatureService/GeoJSON), plus the six-level damage crosswalk.
- Day/night population by building (e.g. LandScan-type) is desirable; the source is UNVERIFIED.

**Logistics (POD / shelter)**
- Needs the impacted population per area (people in Major/Destroyed homes + people without water/power), **within 24 h**, converted to **3 L water + 2 meals per person per day** over a 72–96 h requirement.
- Needs candidate POD sites that are reachable by open roads, sized to the Type I/II/III footprints (250×500 / 250×300 / 150×300 ft) and throughput (20k/10k/5k people/day).
- Needs a shelter-demand estimate (displaced people from Major/Destroyed homes) against 20–40 sq ft per person capacity.

**Utilities / water**
- Needs damaged buildings overlaid on **EAGLE-I county customers-out (15-min)** or ODIN utility feeds, separating "no power because destroyed" from "restorable", **refreshed daily**.
- Needs community water-system service areas (state/SDWIS inventory) shaded by boil-water status, plus a **private-well layer**: damaged or flooded homes outside system service areas. In Helene this was 300k+ people, and those homes are where well-test kits go. Needed **within 72 h**.

**Insurers**
- Needs per-address damage class + confidence + imagery date, with a total-loss candidate flag (Destroyed), as CSV/GeoJSON keyed on lat/lon + address, **within 24–72 h of imagery**. Must disclose that roof and exterior evidence cannot show interior flood depth.

**Survivors**
- Needs a per-address before/after image pair with damage class, date and source, printable as PDF, as supporting evidence. It does not replace the required occupancy, ownership and insurance documents. Must be available **within the 60-day appeal window**.

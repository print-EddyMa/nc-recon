# Launch film v5: the hype cut

72 s, cut to the beat of "Horizons" by Scott Buckley, which plays as one unbroken take and peaks on the logo. `compose_v7.py` holds the timeline. No voice. Sound-off proof: all the information is on screen.

## What the study found

Sampled at 5 and 10 frames from the films themselves (references only; nothing from them is in this film).

| Film | What it does |
|---|---|
| Claude Fable 5, closing | About 30 single objects on paper in 8 s. Shot length falls from 25 frames to 5. Backgrounds are tones of similar brightness. Then a swarm, then the wordmark resolves letter by letter. |
| Claude Fable 5.1 | Paper diagrams drawn in ink and dots, cut into an interview. Closing montage of round specimens. |
| OpenAI GPT-6 Astra | A year rolls up out of a mask ("1979", "2026"). One dot crosses every scene as a through-line. |
| OpenAI Codex, Super Bowl | Match cuts on hands and objects, ending on one word per second on black. |
| Anduril, Fight Unfair | About 16 shots in 27 s, the biggest action first, a small end card. |

## Pacing, measured

Seconds per shot at 1 frame a second: Introducing GPT-5 6.5, GPT-5.5 5.7, Cursor 2.0 4.4, ChatGPT Work 3.1, Claude Fable 5 2.5, GPT-6 Astra 2.5, Anduril Fight Unfair 1.5, Replit Design 1.1, Figure 03 Trailer 1.2. The 58 s cut measured 1.2, as fast as a hardware trailer, so it was slowed toward the product launches.

## What we made of it

- **The pile is the job.** After the storm, someone has to check every building. So 10 real Helene photographs land on a pile while a tally runs to the 80,000 assessments crews did by hand.
- **Black and white, equal brightness**, so the pile reads as one case file.
- **Through-line:** the orange tile from the logo is the period on every line of type, and it is the tile knocked out at the end.
- **Type rolls up out of a mask**, then cuts on the beat.
- **The name resolves letter by letter**, with keystrokes.
- **The sound logo** is the four-note motif from the scan, played on bells over the logo.

## Rules it keeps

- 96% carries its limits on screen: wildfire model, one held-out test, flood not validated.
- The film says "Built after Helene". The post text says it was not used in the response and carries the DVIDS no-endorsement line, the credits and the repo link. The end card stays clean.
- Per-building damage appears only for the showcase home.
- No founders on screen.
- Full-frame flashes stay at three a second or fewer, measured on the finished files: the fastest shots are all paper tones.

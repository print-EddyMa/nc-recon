# Launch film v4: "The scan"

70 s at 100 bpm, one beat is 0.6 s. No voice. For YouTube and X (16:9), Reels and Shorts (9:16), plus a 31 s teaser.

## What we took from the references

| Reference | What we took |
|---|---|
| Anduril, Fury | Near black, a rim-lit object, no words until the end |
| Apple product films | One idea per shot, one fact in big type, hard cuts on the beat |
| Nothing | Two or three words beside the object, a strong type voice |
| Claude, "Keep thinking" | Human stakes first, then one line and the logo |

Pinterest and commercial films were reference only. Everything on screen is ours, public domain or openly licensed.

## Rules it keeps

- NC Recon was built after the storm and was not used in Helene. The end card says so.
- No invented numbers. Every figure is traced in `CREDITS.md`.
- The 96% is shown with what it is: the wildfire model, one held-out test, flood still being validated.
- No per-building damage on imagery except the showcase home.

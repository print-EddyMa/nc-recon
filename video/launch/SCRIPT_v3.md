# Groma launch film v3: script

A story, not a feature list. One place (Swannanoa, North Carolina), one storm
(Helene), the old way of answering "which homes are gone?", and what changes
with Groma. Told in dated text cards over real material, with natural sound
and a sparse score. No narrator: a synthetic voice over real loss reads as
cheap, and the strongest words here are dated facts with sources.

Research behind the choices: `docs/research/` and the session's story research
(Pixar story spine; StoryBrand's split of hero and guide without the sales
beats; documentary intertitles at about 15 characters a second plus a beat;
one typeface; static text that fades; long holds; no HUD, whip pans, grain or
neon, which read as templated or as defence-tech targeting).

## Rules

- **Honest about time.** Groma was built after the storm and tested on Helene data. It was not used in the response, and no card implies it was.
- **Accuracy at the level tested.** Every figure has a source on screen or in CREDITS.md.
- **Privacy.** No per-building damage overlay on aerial imagery, except the one showcase home the public site already shows (17S LV 7253 4080). App screens are from Swannanoa (a tuning area).
- **The people who did the work are the heroes.** Search teams and county staff do the work; Groma is the guide that helps them start in the right place.

## Treatment

- **Type:** one family, Source Serif 4 (OFL). Cards at 52 to 60 px, sentence case, white, left-aligned in the lower left or centred on black. Lower thirds at 24 px in a quieter grey with source and date. No labels, no monospace, no eyebrows, no word-by-word animation: text fades in and out.
- **Picture:** real material held long. Slow drift on photos (at most 4% scale), locked-off 3D. Cuts and half-second dissolves only. Light contrast grade; no vignette or grain added.
- **Sound:** rain and river ambience under the opening, a sparse original score (piano and low strings, synthesised) from the park onward, silence around the proof. No voice.

## YouTube (`ncrecon_launch_v8_A_16x9.mp4`)

**Title:** NC Recon: every building, after the disaster

**Description:**

After Hurricane Helene, more than 100 search teams went house by house and logged 80,000 damage assessments. Then the next storm came.

NC Recon reads after-event imagery and maps every building, the roads likely cut, landing-zone candidates and search sectors. 893 buildings in Swannanoa took 88 seconds on one laptop. People check the hard calls.

It is free and open source (Apache-2.0).

https://github.com/print-EddyMa/nc-recon

What the numbers mean: 96% is the wildfire model's precision on Major or Destroyed calls against CAL FIRE inspections of 2,246 buildings after the Eaton fire (recall 94.2%), one pre-registered held-out test. Flood accuracy is still being validated. NC Recon is decision support: verify on the ground. It was built after the storm and was not used in the Helene response.
